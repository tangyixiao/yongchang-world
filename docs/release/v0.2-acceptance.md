- Jinghai starts with eight frigates, four levels of naval administration using simple sailor recruitment, and a level-two shipyard. The four administration levels provide 4,000 sailor capacity for the eight frigates' 4,000-person crew requirement. Ordinary characters continue to use the base game's culture name pools; the house generation names do not apply to commoners or population groups. Later generated heirs do not yet receive generation-name sequencing; SHU's existing hook only preserves the Li surname.

## Visual clothing boundary

The base game assigns both Han and Manchu cultures `ethnicities = { 1 = asian }` and `graphics = east_asian`, but the direct ruler-clothing rule is in `gfx/portraits/portrait_modifiers/01_clothes.txt`: `chinese_imperial_clothes_trigger` gives an own-country Han-heritage monarch under monarchy or social monarchy the `chinese_imperial_outfits` portrait modifier. That vanilla imperial outfit reads as Qing-style. Pop clothing uses the game's Chinese low/common outfit modifiers by strata. Yongchang World marks its royal characters as Han but does not override these portrait rules or package clothing assets, so the vanilla outfit remains. East Asian Clothes: Redux's 1.0.1 change notes mention correcting Executives in Ming/Han China who wore Qing court outfits, but that does not establish compatibility for this mod's custom rulers; its Steam Workshop page currently shows removed/incompatible status and no local copy is installed. No visual Han/Ming clothing result has been confirmed in-game, and this mod does not claim to provide one.
