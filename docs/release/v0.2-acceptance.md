## Visual clothing boundary

The base game assigns both Han and Manchu cultures `ethnicities = { 1 = asian }` and `graphics = east_asian`, but the direct ruler-clothing rule is in `gfx/portraits/portrait_modifiers/01_clothes.txt`: `chinese_imperial_clothes_trigger` gives an own-country Han-heritage monarch under monarchy or social monarchy the `chinese_imperial_outfits` portrait modifier; it also has a separate `home_country = c:CHI` branch that bypasses the Han-heritage check. That vanilla imperial outfit reads as Qing-style. Pop clothing uses the game's Chinese low/common outfit modifiers by strata.

v0.2 adds a script-level override, without packaging any third-party assets: `yongchang_world/gfx/portraits/portrait_modifiers/ywc_han_royal_appearance.txt` injects three portrait-modifier entries (`INJECT:clothes` → vanilla `chinese_court_outfits`, `INJECT:headgear` → `no_headgear`, `INJECT:hairstyles` → `east_asian_hairstyles`) with weight `add = 2100`, above the vanilla imperial (1100) and court (1000) weights, gated by `ywc_han_royal_appearance_trigger` in `yongchang_world/common/scripted_triggers/ywc_appearance_triggers.txt` (own-country ruler of SHU, DMG, or NMG). SHU/DMG/NMG monarchs therefore fall back to the highest-tier non-imperial Chinese court outfit with no headgear and non-queue hairstyles. MHG 明香 (overseas remnant) is intentionally left on vanilla rules. All referenced vanilla templates were verified to exist (`97_genes_accessories_clothes.txt:139`, `98_genes_accessories_headgear.txt:28`, `02_genes_accessories_hairstyles.txt:285`), and the trigger key carries bilingual localization. No visual in-game confirmation has been made yet; static gates only prove the modifiers parse and the referenced assets exist.
