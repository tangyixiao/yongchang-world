# Yongchang World v0.1 acceptance record

## Baseline and scope

This release candidate targets Victoria 3 **1.13.11 (Matcha)**. It contains the ten-country content slice for SHU, JHG, DMG, NQG, OIR, MNG, TIB, KOR, LAN, and NMG. NMG remains a contested Mexican autonomous subject rather than an instant independent American empire.

There is no planned 1900—1950 political ending: the scenario provides routes and pressures, not a predetermined final political outcome.

没有预定1900—1950政治结局，玩家的路线选择和外部压力共同决定后续局势。

## Known limits

- ~~Most main journals and routes cannot currently complete~~ **Closed**: every one of the 71 journal completion variables now has a setter. All ten countries plus the shared regional journals are wired through real bilingual decision events following the content_catalog success/failure/abandon outcomes contract (DMG, NQG, SHU, JHG, OIR, MNG, TIB, KOR, LAN, NMG; the shared ocean-frontiers journal covers MHG and NMG via `ywc_shared.2`). Cleanup along the way: the duplicate TIB regional journal `ywc_je_highland_without_master` was removed with its content_starts entry, the always-visible defect on the ocean-frontiers journal was narrowed to MHG/NMG, and SHU's two main journals are both wired (`ywc_je_yongchang_century` via `ywc_shu.6`, `ywc_je_eternal_yongchang` via bootstrap). A duplicate-loc regression introduced mid-round was caught by a hidden launch and fixed; the final launch (charters/11) parsed clean.
- The mod does not change underlying map geometry; several regional starts deliberately use Province subsets.
- NQG population, DMG Manila claims, JHG Taiwan boundaries, and NMG's Mexican subject relationship are enforced by the scenario data.
- The 15-run, three-seed, 1846/1866/1900 long observation matrix and visual country-selection checks remain manual acceptance work. They are not represented as completed here because no desktop switching, screenshots, or UI clicks were performed; the step-by-step procedure and evidence formats live in `docs/release/manual-acceptance-playbook.md`.
- Observation summaries are generated only from a sibling `run.json` plus exactly 30 unique `(year, country)` checkpoints per run. The summarizer derives `status=verified`; hand-editing that status in a matrix cannot satisfy the release gate.
- The three DLL families used by the installed game compatibility layer were not modified by this repository.
